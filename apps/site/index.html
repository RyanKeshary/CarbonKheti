Honest Expectations • What We Cannot Promise

Carbon income per smallholder is modest: an average 1 ha paddy generates 1.2 to 1.8 tCO₂e net reduction, yielding approximately ₹1,500 to ₹2,500 per crop season at our contractual floor price of ₹1,200/t. CarbonKheti is not a get-rich-quick promise. The primary, durable household benefits stem from 15%–30% reduced irrigation electricity and diesel costs, root aeration, and free weather-grounded advisory.
